Does your vehicle need a test?

For 2026 registration, Georgia generally requires testing for gasoline cars and light-duty trucks from model years 2002–2023, with a gross vehicle weight rating of 8,500 lb or less, registered in the 13 covered counties, including Rockdale.

Exemptions may apply. Requirements change by registration year; check Georgia’s Clean Air Force guidance for your renewal year.

GOOD TO KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

A few common

Plan ahead with information
for both services.

Can I test my vehicle and renew my tag here?+

Yes. Quick Pass offers emissions testing and has an on-site Georgia MV Express tag-renewal kiosk. Renewal is self-service and requires eligible registration records, including a passing emissions result on file when required. Check the separate service hours before visiting.

What do I need for tag renewal?+

Bring your valid Georgia driver’s license, renewal notice, and vehicle information. Your address must be correct, your county must participate, and required insurance and emissions records must be on file.

Check the state’s kiosk requirements ↗
Is my vehicle eligible for emissions testing?+

Requirements depend on the registration year, model year, fuel type, vehicle weight, and county of registration. Review the year-specific guidance above; exemptions may apply.

Check Georgia’s Clean Air Force requirements ↗
What payment methods are accepted?+

The tag-renewal kiosk accepts major credit and debit cards. {{KIOSK_CASH}}

What happens if my vehicle fails?+

Use your inspection report to arrange appropriate repairs. Complete the Emissions Repair Form, then return for retesting. The original station provides one free retest within the state’s 30-calendar-day window, measured from the initial inspection’s date and time.
